Welcome to Riara

Where every journey begins with a sip

Riara is a family-owned tea farm in the green highlands of Limuru. What started as a farm is now a place where visitors come to learn where Kenyan tea comes from, from the leaf in the field to the cup in your hand.

  • Guided by the farmers and tea makers themselves
  • Hands-on: plant, pick and taste
  • Tours for families, schools, groups and international visitors
  • Pay easily with M-Pesa

More than a tea tour

Tea plantation walks

Walk the estate, learn how tea is grown, and try picking and planting.

Factory visits

Follow green leaf through processing and packing at Maramba Tea Factory.

Tasting & iced tea

Taste our teas at a tasting ceremony and try the famous Riara iced tea.

Avocado orchard

Walk the orchard with a local agronomist and pick fruit when it's in season.

School visits

Tea, the milk parlour, the sheep shed and the orchard: a whole working farm to learn from.

Plant your own tea tree

Plant a seedling labelled with your name and watch it grow into a full tea tree.
