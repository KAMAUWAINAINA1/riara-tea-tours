About us

The family and team who look after the farm, the kitchen and every guest who visits Riara.

Leadership

The family behind Riara

Riara is a family-run farm. These are the people who set its direction and make sure it stays true to its roots.

GK

Grace Koinange

Owner & Chairperson

Grace is the owner of Riara and the keeper of its story. Grace guides the long-term vision for the farm, making sure it remains a family home, a working tea farm and a place where visitors feel welcome.

Kinyenje Ngigi

Chief Executive Officer

Kinyenje leads Riara day to day, from the tours and the tea shop to partnerships with schools, companies and travel operators. The goal: growing Riara into East Africa's home of tea tourism.

Karuga Ngigi

Chief Financial Officer

Karuga oversees Riara's finances, from budgeting and pricing to planning for the farm's future. Karuga's work keeps the business on a sound footing so the farm can keep investing in its people and its land.

Our team

The people who make your visit

Virginia Mwangi

Head Chef

Virginia runs the farmhouse kitchen. Virginia prepares the home-cooked Kenyan meals and teas served to our guests, using fresh produce from the farm and the surrounding Limuru community.

John Kago

Head Farm Manager

John looks after the land: the tea fields, the nursery and the avocado orchard. John leads the farm team through planting, pruning and plucking, and knows every row of tea on the estate.

Kamau Wainaina

Head of IT

Kamau runs Riara's technology, from this website and online bookings to the systems behind the shop. Kamau's work makes it easy for guests to find us, plan a visit and order tea from anywhere.
