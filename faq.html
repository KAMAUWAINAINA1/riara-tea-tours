Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before your visit.

What is Riara Tea Tours?

We run tea tourism and cultural experiences on our family farm in Limuru, near Nairobi. We offer guided tea farm and factory tours, avocado orchard tours and educational visits for schools.

Do I need to book in advance?

Yes. Please book at least 48 hours ahead (24 hours for the Tea Farm Tour). Schools, large groups and custom packages may need more notice.

What tours do you offer?

The Tea Farm Tour, the Tea Factory Tour, the School & Student Tour and the Avocado Orchard Tour. We can also put together custom visits for groups. See all tours and prices.

Are tours suitable for children and older guests?

Absolutely. We adjust the pace and activities for children, older guests and school groups. Just let us know when booking.

What should I bring?

Comfortable clothes and walking shoes, plus a hat or umbrella. Bring a camera and a water bottle. We provide everything you need for planting and picking.

Are meals included?

The Farm Tour starts with tea and a pancake breakfast. Lunch is included on the Factory Tour, the Avocado Orchard Tour and the international Farm Tour with lunch: BBQ chicken, rice, garden salad and red beans, straight from the farm. Other dishes can be added at an extra cost. Let us know about any dietary needs.

Can tours be customised?

Yes. We create packages for individuals, families, companies and schools, from a short visit to a full day on the farm.

How do I get to Riara Farm?

We're in Limuru, about 45 minutes from Nairobi. You can drive, take a taxi or ask us to arrange transport (from KES 3,500 return). Our Google Plus Code is VMVR+6M Limuru. We send detailed directions when you book.

Do you offer group or student discounts?

Students pay KES 1,000 each. On the Tea Factory Tour, children go free when 5 or more adults book together. For other groups, ask us for a quote.

How do I pay?

A 50% deposit by M-Pesa or bank transfer confirms your booking, and you pay the balance on arrival. See our booking policy for cancellations.
