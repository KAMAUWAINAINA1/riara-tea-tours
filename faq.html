Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before your visit.

What is Riara Tea Tours?

We run tea tourism and cultural experiences on our family farm in Limuru, near Nairobi. We offer guided farm and factory tours, tea tasting, nature walks, wellness retreats and educational visits for schools and groups.

Do I need to book in advance?

Yes. Please book at least 48 hours ahead (24 hours for the Tea Farm Tour). Schools, large groups and custom packages may need more notice.

What tours do you offer?

Tea farm tours, factory tours, group and corporate outings, school trips, family retreats and tea & wellness retreats. See all tours and prices.

Are tours suitable for children and older guests?

Absolutely. We adjust the pace and activities for children, older guests and school groups. Just let us know when booking.

What should I bring?

Comfortable clothes and walking shoes, plus a hat or umbrella. Bring a camera and a water bottle. We provide everything you need for blending workshops and retreats.

Are meals included?

Freshly brewed tea is always included. Full-day experiences include lunch, and we can arrange a garden lunch for other tours on request. Let us know about any dietary needs.

Can tours be customised?

Yes. We create packages for individuals, families, companies and schools, from a short visit to a multi-day retreat.

How do I get to Riara Farm?

We're in Limuru, about 45 minutes from Nairobi. You can drive, take a taxi or ask us to arrange transport (from KES 3,500 return). Our Google Plus Code is VMVR+6M Limuru. We send detailed directions when you book.

Do you offer group or student discounts?

Yes. Schools, student groups and groups of 10 or more get special rates. Ask us for a quote.

How do I pay?

A 50% deposit by M-Pesa or bank transfer confirms your booking, and you pay the balance on arrival. See our booking policy for cancellations.
