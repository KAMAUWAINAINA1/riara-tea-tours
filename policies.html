Booking & cancellation

How to book

All tours must be booked in advance. Call or WhatsApp , email , or use our booking form. Please book at least 48 hours ahead.

Payment

A 50% deposit (M-Pesa or bank transfer) confirms your booking, and you pay the balance when you arrive. For large group and custom events we agree payment terms with you directly.

Cancelling or rescheduling

  • More than 7 days before: full refund of your deposit, or reschedule for free.
  • 3–7 days before: the deposit is non-refundable, but you can reschedule.
  • Less than 72 hours before: the deposit is non-refundable.

Late arrivals & no-shows

Tours start on time. If you're running late, please call us and we'll do our best to fit you in. Bookings for guests who don't arrive are non-refundable.

If we have to cancel

If we have to cancel because of extreme weather or other unforeseen events, you can choose a new date or a full refund of your deposit.

Groups

Groups of 10 or more, schools and companies: please contact us so we can agree a package and terms that suit you.

Privacy

We only collect what we need to arrange your visit: usually your name, phone number and email when you contact or book with us. We use it to handle your booking, answer your questions, improve our tours and, only if you ask, send you news.

We never sell or rent your personal information. We only share it when needed to provide our service, for example with a payment provider. You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you. Just contact us.

This website does not use advertising cookies. The embedded map and video are provided by Google and YouTube, and they have their own privacy policies.
